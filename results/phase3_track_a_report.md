# Phase 3 Track A — rolling-origin baselines and Tier 1 models

## Evaluation boundary and design

- Dataset kind: synthetic. Findings mean **method validated on simulated data**, not proven factory savings.
- Backtest origins: 2024-07-01, 2024-10-07, 2025-01-13, 2025-04-21, 2025-07-28, 2025-11-03; fixed horizon: 14 weeks.
- Tuning origins: 2024-07-01, 2024-10-07. Held validation origins: 2025-01-13, 2025-04-21, 2025-07-28, 2025-11-03.
- Tuning uses three seed-42 random-search configurations per LightGBM objective on the same origins and search space. Only the selected configuration per objective is run on the four held validation origins. Baselines use those same held validation origins.
- Fit targets are observed `train_val` demand strictly before/at each origin. Warm-up initializes lag and seasonal-history values, not target-fitting rows. Every scored date is train-validation; final-test/future labels were not evaluated.
- Lead time is the SOB-weighted supplier mean in D2 divided by seven and rounded up (minimum one week). It is available for 158 of 160 demand parts. Parts without a D2 lead time or with lead time longer than the 14-week horizon are omitted from lead-time metrics: 2 missing; 3 over horizon. They remain in weekly metrics.
- Lead-time actual and forecast totals use the same observed weeks in each part-region's first lead-time window. Missing actual weeks are excluded from both sides; denominators are reported.
- `same_period_last_year` and 52-week seasonal naïve are retained as separate requested candidate labels; they use the same 52-week mapping, so their forecasts are mathematically identical.

## Composite candidates

`simple_average_ensemble` is the unweighted mean of nine distinct baseline and LightGBM forecasts; the duplicate same-period-last-year mapping is omitted to avoid double weighting. `sbc_segment_router` uses the fixed project-configured rule: LightGBM Tweedie for Smooth/Erratic, SBA for Intermittent/Lumpy, and the category/type analogue fallback for Unclassified. Segment labels are recomputed from origin-visible history. These are measured candidates, not selected winners; the equal-weight ensemble has no probabilistic output.

## Unclassified-series analogue fallback

The separate `analogue_category_vehicle_fallback` fallback uses only same-part-category, same-vehicle-type donor parts visible at the origin; same-region donors are preferred when available. Each donor's rate is the mean observed train-validation demand over the trailing 13 calendar weeks, and the fallback is the median donor rate. The target series is excluded as its own donor. This broader group was selected because the supplied catalogue has unique component/vehicle-type combinations per part, leaving the original exact group with no alternate-part donors. ## Metric interpretation

Metrics follow `SPEC.md`. Weekly and lead-time levels, origin, segment and horizon-step records are in `results/track_a_candidates.csv`. Missing-target rows are excluded, genuine zero demand is retained, and undefined zero-total WAPE/bias remain blank. MASE uses adjacent observed train-validation one-step naïve MAE per series at that origin. The sMAPE zero/zero contribution is zero. Point-only models have no quantile calibration output.

## Limitations

These are synthetic-data results from six rolling origins; tuning uses two early train-validation origins and held validation uses four later origins. Dependence remains across parts and origins, and there is no real operational cost or external demand evidence. Phase 4 will apply the frozen weighted selection, sensitivity, paired comparisons, and champion freeze. The final test remains sealed for Phase 7.

## Reproduction

Run `python -m src.forecasting.backtest` from the repository root with dependencies in `requirements.txt` installed. Seed: 42.

## Phase 4 correction to lead-time aggregation

The original lead-time aggregator could count missing baseline forecasts as zero. Phase 4 excludes incomplete lead-time windows and recomputes metrics from the saved held-validation forecasts. The corrected values supersede the earlier pooled lead-time rows; see `track_a_phase4_metric_correction.csv` for before/after values. The maximum absolute WAPE change was 0.002436 percentage points.
